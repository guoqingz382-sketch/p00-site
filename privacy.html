隐私政策

最近修订：2026 年 9 月 16 日 ｜ 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 16 日

「相见有时」是一款在你的设备本地使用的日子记录与提醒工具。我们不收集你的任何个人信息。

一、我们收集什么

我们不收集你的任何个人信息：

  • 你创建的内容（日子名称、日期、农历设置、备注等）仅保存在你的设备本地，我们不上传、不读取、不备份到任何服务器。
  • 不要求注册：本 App 无账号体系，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录等任何身份信息。
  • 无第三方 SDK：本 App 不含广告 SDK、统计分析 SDK 或任何埋点组件。
  • 无内购：当前版本不提供任何付费功能，因此不涉及任何支付信息处理。

二、权限与用途

本 App 仅申请以下权限，且都只在设备本地生效：

  • 通知权限：用于在你设定的日子到达时发送本地提醒。提醒在你的设备本地生成，不经过任何服务器。你可以随时在系统「设置」中关闭。
  • 相册（仅写入）权限：仅在你主动选择把 App 生成的内容保存到相册时使用。本 App 只申请「添加」权限，不申请读取权限——我们无法查看、读取或上传你相册中的任何内容。
    当前首发版本未使用相册权限；后续版本支持把纪念日卡片保存到相册时启用，届时同步更新本政策。

拒绝授权的后果：即使你拒绝任一权限，App 的核心功能（记录日子、查看天数）仍可正常使用。

三、数据存储与安全

  • 所有数据仅保存在你的设备上（本地数据库与本地文件）。
  • 你可以随时在 App 内修改或删除任意条目。
  • 删除 App 即删除全部数据；App 内删除某条记录会同时清除其对应的提醒。

四、第三方共享

  • 我们不向任何第三方出售、出租或共享你的任何数据。
  • 当你主动使用系统分享面板把 App 生成的内容发送给他人（如微信、信息）时，分享行为由你本人发起、由 iOS 系统完成，我们不经手、不留存。（当前首发版本暂无内容分享入口。）

五、儿童隐私

本 App 面向一般用户，不针对 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不定向收集儿童个人信息。

六、你的权利

  • 你可以随时查看、修改、删除你在本 App 内创建的全部内容。
  • 由于数据仅存于本地，卸载本 App 即完成全部数据的彻底删除。
  • 若你对数据处理有任何疑问，可通过下方方式联系我们。

七、未来功能与政策更新

  • 我们可能在后续版本引入需要联网的可选功能（例如：你主动选择后，与他人共享你指定的某个日子）。此类功能上线前，我们会先更新本隐私政策，并确保：由你主动选择开启、默认关闭；仅处理你明确选择共享的那部分内容；不因此收集你的身份信息。
  • 本政策如有变更，我们会在本页面更新并标注最新修订日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026 ｜ Effective: September 16, 2026

Till We Meet is a day-tracking and reminder app that works entirely on your device. We do not collect any personal information.

1. What we collect

We collect no personal information whatsoever:

  • Content you create (day names, dates, lunar calendar settings, notes) is stored only on your device. We do not upload, read, or back it up to any server.
  • No registration: there are no accounts, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, or contacts.
  • No third-party SDKs: no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no tracking of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases: the current version has no paid features, so no payment information is processed.

2. Permissions and how they are used

The app requests only the following permissions, all of which operate locally on your device:

  • Notifications: used to send local reminders when a day you set arrives. Reminders are generated on your device and never pass through a server. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Photos (add-only): used only when you choose to save something the app generates to your photo library. The app requests add-only access, never read access — we cannot view, read, or upload anything in your photo library.
    The initial release does not use photo library access; it will be enabled in a later version that supports saving anniversary cards, at which point this policy will be updated.

If you decline: the core features (recording days, viewing counts) still work normally.

3. Storage and security

  • All data is stored on your device only (local database and local files).
  • You can edit or delete any entry inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app deletes all data; deleting an entry inside the app also removes its associated reminder.

4. Sharing with third parties

  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • If you choose to send content through the system share sheet, that action is initiated by you and handled by iOS. We neither process nor retain it. (The initial release has no share entry point.)

5. Children's privacy

This app is intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

  • You can view, edit, and delete all content you created in the app at any time.
  • Because data lives only on your device, uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything.
  • If you have any questions about data handling, contact us using the details below.

7. Future features and policy updates

  • We may introduce optional networked features in future versions (for example, sharing a specific day with someone at your request). Before any such feature ships, we will update this policy first, and it will be opt-in and off by default, process only the content you explicitly choose to share, and never collect your identity.
  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and note the revision date.
