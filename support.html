常见问题

怎么添加一个新的日子？

打开 App，在首页右下角点「＋」，填写名称和日期即可。农历生日或传统节日，把日期切换到「农历」再选。

为什么没有收到提醒？

提醒依赖系统通知权限，请依次检查：
① 首次使用时是否允许了「通知」；
② 若曾拒绝，前往 iPhone「设置 → 通知 → 相见有时」，打开「允许通知」；
③ 确认该日子还没过去——已经过去的日子不会再提醒。

可以提前几天提醒吗？

可以。编辑某个日子时，在「提醒」里选择「当天」「提前 1 天」或「提前 3 天」。当天一定会提醒，勾选提前只是在当天之外多加一次，不会替代当天那条。若提前的那一天已经过去，App 会提示你，并且不会补发。

桌面小组件怎么加？

长按手机桌面空白处 → 左上角「＋」→ 搜索「相见有时」→ 选择小号或中号，添加到桌面。「最近日子」小组件不需要你手动选，它会自动显示离你最近的日子：小号显示最近 1 条，中号列出最近 3 条。

换了主题色，小组件没跟着变？

小组件的刷新由 iOS 系统调度，不是实时的，通常几秒到几十秒内会更新。如果较长时间没变，可以重新进入一次 App，或把小组件移除后重新添加。

农历（阴历）准确吗？

支持农历含闰月推算，农历生日、春节、中秋这类节日都按农历计算，勾选「忽略年份」后每年自动到来。内置的中国节日与墨西哥节日也可以一键导入，日期会自动填好。

我的数据存在哪里？会丢吗？

所有数据只保存在你的手机上，不上传、不联网、不同步到任何服务器。好处是完全私密；代价是卸载 App 会连同数据一起删除，且当前版本没有云备份或导出功能。如果有特别重要的日子，建议同时在自己的日历里记一份。

支持哪些语言？

中文、English、Español 三种，跟随系统语言自动切换，不需要手动设置。

App 收费吗？有广告吗？

当前版本完全免费，没有内购、没有订阅、没有广告。

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a day?

Open the app and tap the "＋" button in the bottom-right corner of the home screen, then enter a name and date. For lunar-calendar birthdays or traditional festivals, switch the date picker to Lunar first.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Reminders need notification permission. Please check:
① whether you allowed notifications on first launch;
② if you declined before, go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Till We Meet and turn on "Allow Notifications";
③ whether the day has already passed — reminders are not sent for past dates.

Can reminders come earlier?

Yes. When editing a day, choose "On the day", "1 day before", or "3 days before". The day itself always reminds you — picking an advance reminder adds one extra notification rather than replacing it. If that earlier date has already passed, the app will tell you and will not send a catch-up notification.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen → tap "＋" → search for "Till We Meet" → pick the small or medium size. The "Nearest day" widget needs no manual selection: it automatically shows whichever day is coming up soonest — one item in the small size, three in the medium size.

I changed the theme color but the widget didn't change.

Widget refreshes are scheduled by iOS and are not instant — usually a few seconds to under a minute. If it still looks unchanged after a while, open the app once, or remove the widget and add it back.

Is the lunar calendar accurate?

Yes, including leap months. Lunar birthdays and festivals such as Chinese New Year and Mid-Autumn are calculated on the lunar calendar, and with "ignore year" enabled they recur automatically each year. Built-in Chinese and Mexican holidays can also be imported in one tap.

Where is my data stored? Can I lose it?

All data is stored only on your phone — nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent to any server. The upside is complete privacy; the trade-off is that deleting the app also deletes the data, and the current version has no cloud backup or export. If a day really matters, consider keeping a copy in your own calendar too.

Which languages are supported?

Chinese, English, and Spanish, following your system language automatically — no setup needed.

Does the app cost anything? Are there ads?

The current version is completely free: no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads.
